Program snapshots

Johns Hopkins

Baltimore, Maryland · MD ~4.75% + Baltimore City ~3.2%

A historic powerhouse with one of the largest neurology departments in the country, deep subspecialty bench strength and a research engine that opens doors everywhere.

Academic NeurologistFuture ResearcherFellowship-Oriented Applicant

Strengths

  • Name recognition that carries into any fellowship or faculty search
  • Depth in nearly every subspecialty, including neuroimmunology and neuromuscular
  • Huge, complex inpatient volume
  • Strong physician-scientist pathways

Tradeoffs

  • Demanding culture with a reputation for long hours
  • Baltimore neighborhood safety varies a lot block to block
  • Big program can feel less personal without proactive mentoring

Penn (HUP)

Philadelphia, Pennsylvania · PA 3.07% + Philadelphia wage tax ~3.75%

Elite, broad department at HUP with outstanding subspecialty depth, especially cognitive, movement and epilepsy, in a lively and still relatively affordable big city.

Academic NeurologistFuture ResearcherFellowship-Oriented Applicant

Strengths

  • Top-tier research across cognitive, movement disorders and epilepsy
  • Collegial reputation for a program of its size
  • Philadelphia: big-city food and culture at lower cost than NYC or Boston
  • Easy train access to NYC and DC

Tradeoffs

  • Philadelphia wage tax on top of state tax
  • High-acuity, busy services
  • Some neighborhoods feel less safe

Columbia

New York, New York · NY ~6.3% + NYC ~3.9%

The Neurological Institute of New York: a storied department with enormous tertiary referral volume and top research, in Washington Heights.

Academic NeurologistFuture ResearcherFellowship-Oriented Applicant

Strengths

  • Historic flagship neurology institute
  • Massive and diverse patient population
  • Strong stroke, neuro ICU and neuromuscular programs
  • New York's unmatched culture and dining

Tradeoffs

  • Highest cost of living on the list; salary does not fully offset it
  • Commute and housing logistics in Manhattan
  • Busy, high-intensity training

Weill Cornell

New York, New York · NY ~6.3% + NYC ~3.9%

NewYork-Presbyterian/Weill Cornell on the Upper East Side: a smaller-feeling academic program inside a major NYC system, with strong neuro-oncology and headache.

Academic NeurologistLifestyle-Focused Resident (NYC style)Fellowship-Oriented Applicant

Strengths

  • Close-knit class size within a big-name system
  • Upper East Side location with subsidized housing options (verify)
  • Strong ties to Memorial Sloan Kettering for neuro-oncology
  • Shares NYP network with Columbia

Tradeoffs

  • Very high cost of living
  • Smaller program means less volume in some niches
  • NYC pace isn't for everyone

NYU Langone

New York, New York · NY ~6.3% + NYC ~3.9%

NYU Langone is a fast-rising academic center with a renowned comprehensive epilepsy center, strong MS care and Bellevue's public-hospital volume.

Academic NeurologistEpilepsy-FocusedFellowship-Oriented Applicant

  • Bellevue offers safety-net, high-pathology training
  • Modern facilities and strong institutional resources
  • Excellent fellowship pipeline

Tradeoffs

  • Multiple sites can mean a lot of moving around
  • Busy services

Mount Sinai

New York, New York · NY ~6.3% + NYC ~3.9%
